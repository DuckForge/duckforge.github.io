🗡️ Quick-Play Design

Jump in for 5 minutes or 5 hours — your call

Engage in turn-based combat where your decisions make the difference between victory and defeat.

🧙‍♂️ Endless Leveling*

Farm XP forever! (*up to level 8,200)

Watch that little bar grow as you defeat enemies and earn experience. Leveling up gives you stat points to allocate and increases your health and mana. Continue leveling forever with no level cap… sort of. Your character can continue to level as long as you continue earning experience. If you reach level 8,200 you will not be able to earn more experience.

⚔️ Turn‑Based Combat

Tactical choice: sword, spell, or brawn.

Stalwart Fighter using Swords and Shields or Greatswords.

Magickal Adept using Wands and magical Orbs or Staves.

Ferocious Warrior using Axes and Hatchets or Battleaxes.

💎 Loot Transmogrifier

Trade duplicates for mythic gear.

😈 Boss Key

Hide your dungeon with one tap.

We get it. Sometimes you don’t want someone else to see that you’re playing a game. Instead of minimizing the game, hit the Boss Key! The screen will change to something more productivity friendly like a web browser with too many tabs open or a spreadsheet.

🖥️ Casual Windowed Play

8:3 aspect ratio fits perfectly beside work.

Leave it running while other programs and games are active. During downtime in your other games, fight, level up, and loot!

Find Loot

Inspired by many classic ARPG games, find randomly generated stats on items of different rarity. Find the best ones to maximize your gear and bonuses.

Level Up and Improve Loot

The transmogrifier will happily take your money in exchange for helping you to update the level of your item or replace bonuses to get the ultimate loot!

Upgrade Loot

Use Magical Reforging to upgrade lower rarity items into a single higher rarity item.

Store

Buy and sell items you collect along the way that aren’t useful. Take that money and do something good with it! The store gives you access to random items to supplement what you find.

Stash

Shared inventory space between your three characters that lets you transfer items between them.
