Many Ducks Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Many Ducks is developed by Duck Forge, an independent game studio based in Canada. We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information needed to develop and operate our game.

Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, we may collect:

  • Player identifiers: Platform account identifiers, such as SteamID, used to associate gameplay data with a player and support online features.
  • IP addresses: PlayFab processes IP addresses when players connect to its services.
  • Gameplay data: Level progression, feature usage, duck collection, and other in-game activities.
  • Duck trading data: Information needed to create and process one-time trade codes.

How We Use Information

We use this information to understand how players experience Many Ducks, improve gameplay and game balance, identify technical issues, and operate online duck trading. We do not sell your personal information or use gameplay telemetry for targeted advertising.

Cross Platform Duck Trading

Many Ducks allows players to trade ducks using one-time trade codes. A player generates a code and shares it outside the game. Another player can redeem the code to complete the trade.

Duck trading does not include player-to-player chat or messaging. Players are not shown each other's platform account identifiers or other identifying account information through the trading feature. We process the information needed to validate trade codes and complete exchanges, including the ducks being traded.

Third-Party Services

We use Microsoft PlayFab to support gameplay telemetry and online features. The platforms and storefronts through which Many Ducks is distributed may also process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

Information handled by our service providers may be stored or processed outside Canada, depending on their infrastructure and configuration.

Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You can choose whether to enable optional gameplay telemetry and online duck trading through the game's privacy settings, where available.

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, or deletion of your personal information.
