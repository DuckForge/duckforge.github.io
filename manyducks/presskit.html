Description

Many Ducks is a cozy creature-collecting life sim where you win wild ducks over with music. Explore the woods, grow your flock, make your ranch your own, and raise a flock worthy of leading the Duck Parade.

Welcome to Waddleton Cove, a cozy seaside town where ducks roam freely and the annual Duck Parade is the highlight of the season. As the town’s newest Duck Rancher, you’ll build a thriving ranch, discover rare ducks, and raise a flock unlike any other.

Explore shifting forests, collect strange and wonderful ducks, customize your ranch, and prepare your favorite companions for the grand Duck Parade.

Early development: Many Ducks is in its very early stages. Gameplay systems are being refined and the art direction is still evolving, so expect features and visuals to change as Waddleton Cove grows.

Features

Collect Rare Ducks

  • Discover wild ducks with unique appearances, personalities, traits, and songs
  • Hatch rare breeds and grow your flock over time
  • Find hidden ducks, special eggs, and mysterious discoveries throughout the world

Customize Your Ranch and Character

  • Customize your character with cozy outfits and accessories
  • Decorate your ranch and upgrade coops, feeding stations, and specialized habitats to attract even more ducks

Explore the Wilds

  • Venture through ever-changing forests with your duck companions
  • Use your ducks’ unique songs to recruit wild ducks

Celebrate Duck Parade Season

  • Craft parade floats, take part in seasonal festivals, and choose your star duck to lead the annual Duck Parade

Meet the Town of Waddleton Cove

  • Befriend quirky townsfolk, uncover special quests, and discover rare ducks through the relationships you build around town

About Duck Forge

Duck Forge is a fully remote indie studio, mostly located in Canada, created by industry veterans who want to work on what they’re passionate about. Or at least what their four-legged overlords are passionate about.

The studio is currently forging two games: Just Keep Looting, a 3D turn-based dungeon crawler made to play in a window while you wait, and Many Ducks.
